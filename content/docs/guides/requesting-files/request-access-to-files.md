# Request access to another lab's files

If you would like analyze data from another lab you can request access through the Data Catalog.

> [!WARNING]
**The Permissions required for this operation are Lab Director or Bioinformatics User**

1. Click **Data** **Catalog** in the sidebar
1. Browse or search for the sequence files
1. Select the specific files you want
1. Click **Create Analytical Dataset**
1. Add a Dataset Title and Description. Then select your lab and project that is requesting the data.
1. Click Submit for Approval

The Lab Director who owns those files will receive a notification. You'll be notified by email and in-app when they make a decision. If approved, the files will appear in your project.
